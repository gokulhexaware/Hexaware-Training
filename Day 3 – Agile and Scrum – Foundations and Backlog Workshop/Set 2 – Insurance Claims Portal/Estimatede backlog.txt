### 3. Story Point Estimation Technique

Planning Poker is an estimation technique used by Agile Scrum teams to estimate the complexity, effort and uncertainty involved in completing user stories.

The team uses Fibonacci-style values:

**1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13**

Each team member independently selects an estimate. The team discusses differences and agrees on a final story point value.

### 4. Planning Poker Example

**User Story:** IC-03 – Submit Insurance Claim

Three developers provided different estimates:

- Developer 1: 5 Story Points
- Developer 2: 8 Story Points
- Developer 3: 13 Story Points

**Discussion:** The team discussed the complexity of claim submission, mandatory field validation, insurance policy eligibility checks and generation of a unique claim reference number.

**Final Estimate:** 8 Story Points.

The team agreed on 8 points because the story involves several validations and business rules, but its scope is manageable within the planned development work.

### 5. Total Backlog Estimation

The story point estimates for all 12 user stories were added together.

**Total Estimated Backlog = 45 Story Points**

### 6. Conclusion

The InsureAssist Product Backlog contains 12 user stories grouped under four Epics. Each story has been prioritised based on business importance and estimated using Planning Poker. The total estimated backlog size is 45 Story Points. This estimation helps the Scrum team plan Sprints, allocate work and track project progress effectively.
