# Scrum Roles, Events and Artifacts – InsureAssist

## 1. Scrum Roles

**Product Owner:** Responsible for defining the product vision, maintaining the Product Backlog and prioritising user stories according to business value.

**Scrum Master:** Ensures that Scrum practices are followed, facilitates Scrum events and helps the team remove obstacles.

**Developers:** Responsible for designing, developing, testing and delivering the product increment during each Sprint.

## 2. Scrum Events

**Sprint:** A fixed period during which the team works to complete selected backlog items.

**Sprint Planning:** The team discusses the Sprint Goal and selects user stories from the Product Backlog.

**Daily Scrum:** A 15-minute meeting where developers discuss progress, plans and obstacles.

**Sprint Review:** The team demonstrates completed functionality to stakeholders and collects feedback.

**Sprint Retrospective:** The team discusses what went well, what could be improved and how to improve the next Sprint.

## 3. Scrum Artifacts

**Product Backlog:** A prioritised list of all features, improvements and requirements of the Insurance Claims Portal.

**Sprint Backlog:** The selected user stories and work planned for completion during a Sprint.

**Increment:** The completed and usable functionality delivered at the end of a Sprint.

## 4. Artifact Commitments

**Product Goal:** The long-term objective of providing a secure and efficient Insurance Claims Portal.

**Sprint Goal:** The specific objective the team aims to achieve during a Sprint.

**Definition of Done:** The quality criteria that must be satisfied before a user story can be considered complete.

## Conclusion

Scrum helps the InsureAssist team deliver the Insurance Claims Portal incrementally, improve collaboration, receive regular stakeholder feedback and maintain product quality.
